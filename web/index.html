Read the evidence with its limits.
Full training uses eligible labeled training clips while keeping validation and five test views held out. VOA is unlabeled and excluded from supervised training. Clips beyond 30 seconds and overlong training labels are excluded rather than truncated.
WER depends on Myanmar word spacing. Raw CER retains whitespace; no-space CER removes it. These metrics retain punctuation and use NFC normalization. YODAS video overlap and uncertain UCSY speaker identities prevent claims of speaker- or video-disjoint evaluation.
